An astronaut dress-up game

SpaceSuit Explorer

Dress an Astronaut. Prepare for Discovery.

Pick your astronaut, choose a mission and put together the right spacesuit. Check your gear, finish the safety checklist and head out on six adventures, from spacewalks to the Moon!

A learning game for ages 6–12. Not affiliated with or endorsed by NASA. Progress is saved only in this browser.

How to Play

  1. 1
    Choose a mission. Each mission card shows where you are going and what you'll learn. Finish a mission to unlock the next one.
  2. 2
    Dress your astronaut. Drag gear onto the astronaut, or tap an item and press Put on. Tap an item to read what it does and whether it fits the mission.
  3. 3
    Press "Check My Suit". Missing or wrong gear is circled in red. Read why it matters, ask for a hint, fix it and check again.
  4. 4
    Inspect your equipment. Read each gauge. Is it good, or does it need fixing?
  5. 5
    Finish the safety checklist. Answer each question. Wrong answers explain why, so try again!
  6. 6
    Play the mission and earn points and badges. Safe choices earn points, and fixing a mistake earns points too.

Keyboard: use Tab to move, Enter or Space to press. In the Moon mission you can also use the arrow keys to walk.
